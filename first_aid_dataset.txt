### EACIS EMERGENCY MEDICAL DATASET - V1.0 ###

[DOMAIN: FLASH FLOOD SURVIVAL]
- THREAT: Contaminated Water. 
- ACTION: Do not wade in floodwater. If exposed, wash skin with soap immediately. 
- RISK: Open wounds exposed to floodwater require antiseptic and immediate covering.

[DOMAIN: TRAUMA & BLEEDING]
- THREAT: Active Hemorrhage.
- ACTION: Apply firm, direct pressure using the cleanest available cloth. Do not remove soaked bandages; add more on top.
- POSITION: Elevate the injured limb above heart level if possible.

[DOMAIN: FRACTURES]
- THREAT: Broken Bone.
- ACTION: Immobilize the area. Use sticks or folded cardboard as temporary splints.
- CAUTION: Do not attempt to "straighten" or "reset" the bone.

[DOMAIN: HYPOTHERMIA/SHOCK]
- THREAT: Core Temperature Drop.
- ACTION: Remove wet clothing. Wrap in dry blankets or emergency foil.
- POSITION: Lay the patient flat with feet slightly elevated.
